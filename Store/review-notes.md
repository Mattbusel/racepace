# App Review notes

---

No account, login or network connection is required.

PRIVACY: no data is collected. Everything is stored on the device in the app's Documents folder. No account, analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.

HOW TO USE: The Pace tab (lane 1) is the calculator: pick a distance, tap the clock to set a goal time, and the pace is shown per mile or km (MI/KM toggle); the +/- buttons change pace by 5 seconds. Splits (lane 2) lists every split with its pace and clock time. Predict (lane 3) estimates times for other distances from a recent race (Riegel's formula). Races (lane 4) saves races. The round stopwatch button in the middle opens race-day mode: tap GO at the start, then tap once at each marker to see ahead/behind.

HEALTH INFORMATION: Fueling suggestions (Pro) cite the ACSM joint position statement (Thomas, Erdman and Burke 2016, doi 10.1249/MSS.0000000000000852) and Jeukendrup 2014 (doi 10.1007/s40279-014-0148-z); the predictor cites Riegel 1981 (American Scientist 69(3):285-290). Sources are linked under the fueling settings and on the Predict tab. The app states these are general guidance, not medical advice.

IN-APP PURCHASE: the app is free. There is one non-consumable purchase, Race Pace Pro (com.mattbusel.racepace.pro). Free: calculator, even splits, predictor. Pro: race-day mode, split strategies, fueling, pace band PDF and share card, saved races. To see the paywall: tap the stopwatch button in the tab bar, or "Pace band" or "Race day" on the Splits tab, or any strategy other than Even, or Add on the Races tab. Restore purchase is on the paywall and on the Race Pace Pro card at the bottom of the Races tab.

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
A pace and split planner for recreational runners preparing for races. General audience; rated 4+.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS
No setup, login or credentials.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
None. No network requests, analytics, advertising or third-party frameworks. SwiftUI and StoreKit 2.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Miles or kilometres.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / PROTECTED MATERIAL
Not applicable. All art, text and code are my own work.
